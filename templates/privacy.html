Privacy

Last updated: July 22, 2026.

Content you convert

Markdown is sent to a serverless API and processed in memory to produce HTML. The application does not use a document database and does not intentionally store submitted content. Vercel, the hosting provider, may process standard request metadata under its own policies.

Browser storage

The editor saves your draft and settings in local browser storage to protect against accidental refreshes. This data remains on your device until you clear the editor, clear site data, or disable browser storage.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

This site uses Google AdSense publisher code to verify and, after approval, monetize the site. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, local storage, IP addresses, device information, and browsing activity to provide, measure, personalize, or limit advertising. Consent and privacy choices are presented where required through Google Privacy & Messaging. Visible ads remain disabled until the site is approved.

Your choices

Do not submit secrets or highly sensitive personal information. You can clear the editor and your browser's site data at any time. For privacy questions, use the support page.
