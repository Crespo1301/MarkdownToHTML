# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers, writers, bloggers, content teams, and people moving AI-generated
Markdown into websites or content management systems. They arrive with content
ready to convert and need the editor, preview, and HTML output immediately.

## Product Purpose

Provide a free, secure, fast Markdown-to-HTML converter as a public CSolutions
utility. Success means users can confidently paste or upload Markdown, inspect
the rendered result and source, and copy a fragment or download a complete HTML
document without losing work or exposing the preview to active content.

## Brand Personality

Focused, professional, and practical. Copy should be direct and educational,
with enough explanation to help non-specialists without slowing down experienced
users.

## Anti-references

Do not turn the converter into a marketing landing page. Avoid oversized hero
copy, gradients, glass effects, floating decoration, excessive cards, novelty
motion, fake social proof, keyword stuffing, and advertising that resembles or
interrupts tool controls.

## Design Principles

1. Put the working converter first and keep its primary actions visible.
2. Make output modes and actions unambiguous, especially fragment copy versus
   complete-document download.
3. Earn trust through safe defaults, accurate privacy language, transparent
   limitations, and clear error states.
4. Use restrained visual hierarchy so the interface disappears into the task.
5. Add crawlable educational content after the tool, not in front of it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support full keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic
labels and live status announcements, screen readers, 200% zoom, reduced-motion
preferences, sufficient contrast, 44-pixel touch targets, and reliable layouts
from 360-pixel mobile screens through wide desktop screens.
